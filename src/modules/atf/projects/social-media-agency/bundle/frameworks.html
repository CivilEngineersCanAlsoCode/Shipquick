Content Methods

Source: content-methods.csv

num category method_name description output_pattern
1 ideation Audience Pain Mining Scan audience comments/DMs/forums for recurring frustrations. Map each pain to content pillar. Extract 3 angles per pain. Works for any channel. pain points → pillar mapping → 3 content angles
2 ideation Trend Hijacking Find trending topic in your industry. Connect it to your expertise/experience. Ride the wave before it crashes. trending topic → your angle → timely content
3 ideation Experience Reverse Engineering Start from YOUR experience. Ask: what did I learn? Who else needs this? What format fits best? experience → lesson → audience → format
4 ideation Contrarian Flip Take popular advice in your niche. Question it. Find where it breaks. Write the counter-argument. popular belief → holes → your take
5 ideation Question Mining Collect questions people ask you repeatedly (DMs, meetings, mentoring). Each question = 1 post. repeated questions → answer posts
6 ideation Content Collision Combine 2 unrelated pillars (e.g., PM + music, startup + cricket). Unexpected combos create viral potential. pillar A + pillar B → unexpected angle
7 research Competitor Shadow Study Pick 3-5 top creators in your niche. Analyze their top 10 posts. What patterns emerge? Replicate the pattern not the content. competitors → top posts → patterns → your version
8 research Comment Section Gold Mining Read comments on viral posts in your niche. People reveal real opinions objections stories in comments. Each gold comment = content seed. viral post → comments → insights → content seeds
9 research Data Point Hunting Find 1 surprising stat about your topic. Build entire content around that single data point. Stats stop the scroll. topic → surprising stat → context → take
10 research Audience Language Mirroring Study exact words your audience uses to describe their problems. Use THEIR words not industry jargon. Resonance > sophistication. audience words → mirror in content → instant relatability
11 research Trend Radar Sweep Weekly scan: Google Trends Twitter trending LinkedIn news Reddit threads newsletter opens. Map what is hot to your pillars. platforms → trending topics → pillar fit → content queue
12 research Gap Analysis Find what top creators in your niche are NOT talking about. The gap = your opportunity. Underserved topics = lower competition higher loyalty. niche scan → missing topics → your blue ocean
13 hook Hook Stress Test Write same hook in 5 different styles (question bold data story confession). Pick the one with maximum curiosity gap for target channel. topic → 5 hooks → evaluate → winner
14 hook Two-Line Cliffhanger Craft exactly 2 lines that force the reader to click see more. Line 1 = setup. Line 2 = incomplete payoff. The gap does the work. setup → incomplete payoff → curiosity gap
15 hook Pattern Interrupt Break the expected feed pattern. If everyone writes long go ultra-short. If everyone is positive go raw honest. Scroll stops at surprise. feed norm → opposite → attention
16 hook Specificity Injection Replace vague hooks with hyper-specific details. Not 'I failed' but 'I lost Rs 4.7 lakhs in 47 days'. Specifics = credibility = clicks. vague → specific numbers/names/dates → trust
17 hook Identity Hook Start by calling out exactly who should read this. 'If you are a PM who...' Self-identification makes people feel personally addressed. target identity → their specific pain → pull
18 hook Open Loop Start with the END of the story. 'That one decision made me Rs 20L.' Reader needs to know HOW. Do not close the loop until the end. result first → mystery → reveal at end
19 narrative Experience Bridge Connect personal experience to universal insight. MY story → YOUR takeaway. The bridge between 'this happened to me' and 'this matters to you'. personal → bridge → universal takeaway
20 narrative Tension Arc Every good post needs tension. Setup the stakes early. What could go wrong? What was at risk? No tension = no engagement. stakes → rising tension → resolution → lesson
21 narrative Show Don't Tell Replace 'I was scared' with 'My hands were shaking on the keyboard at 2 AM'. Sensory details > abstract emotions. Works for any format. abstract emotion → concrete scene → reader feels it
22 narrative One Post One Point Resist the urge to cover 5 things. One clear point deeply explored. Reader should be able to summarize your post in 1 sentence. multiple ideas → pick ONE → go deep → clarity
23 narrative Bookend Technique Start and end with the same image/phrase/scene but with changed meaning. Creates satisfying closure. opening image → journey → same image new meaning
24 narrative Villain-Victim-Hero Flip Start as the villain or victim of your own story. Then flip to hero through self-awareness. Relatable > aspirational. villain/victim → realization → growth → hero
25 repurposing Content Waterfall One idea flows down: long-form blog → LinkedIn post → carousel slides → reel script → tweet thread → email newsletter. Single input 6 outputs. idea → expand → compress → visualize → micro → email
26 repurposing Format Swap Take an existing post that performed well. Rewrite in completely different format. Story that worked? Make it a listicle. top post → new format → fresh reach
27 repurposing Platform Native Translation Same message native to each platform's culture. LinkedIn = professional insight. Instagram = visual + casual. Substack = deep dive. Not copy-paste — translate. core message → platform culture → native version
28 repurposing Evergreen Recycler Posts older than 90 days with high engagement = recyclable. Update with new data/experience. Repost. Your new audience hasn't seen it. old top post → update → new context → repost
29 repurposing Thread-to-Monologue Take a multi-point post/thread. Pick the ONE point that got most comments. Expand just that into full standalone content. multi-point → top comment point → expand → standalone
30 repurposing Audience Question Flip Someone asks you a great question in comments/DMs. Your reply = next post. The question becomes the hook. Built-in social proof. DM/comment question → hook → detailed answer post
31 engagement Controversy Calibration Find the sweet spot between 'everyone agrees' (boring) and 'everyone is offended' (toxic). Mildly controversial = most comments. safe take → find edge → calibrate → debate magnet
32 engagement Reply Bait Architecture Design the post so people HAVE to respond. Leave a gap. Ask 'what would you do?' Make them complete YOUR sentence. statement → intentional gap → reader fills it
33 engagement Tribal Signal Write for a specific tribe. 'Only PMs will understand this.' Exclusivity drives shares within the tribe. People tag their people. tribe identity → insider reference → tag behavior
34 engagement First Comment Strategy Your first comment on your own post = engagement hack. Add context ask a follow-up share a bonus tip. Kickstarts the comment thread. post live → self-comment → conversation starter
35 engagement Save-Worthy Formatting Make content people bookmark/save. Frameworks checklists templates cheat sheets. Saves signal 'high value' to algorithms across all platforms. actionable content → structured format → save trigger
36 engagement Emotional Peak Placement Place your most emotional line at 60-70% through the post. Reader is invested by then. That is where the Hindi line the emoji the gut punch lands hardest. buildup → peak at 60-70% → release → CTA
37 positioning Intersection Identity You are not just 'a PM'. You are 'IIT Civil Eng → Amex PM → startup investor'. The intersection of your identities IS your brand. Nobody else has your exact combination. identity 1 + identity 2 + identity 3 → unique position
38 positioning Anti-Guru Positioning Do not be the expert who has all answers. Be the practitioner who shares what they are learning. 'I am figuring this out too' > 'Let me teach you'. expert pedestal → come down → peer level → trust
39 positioning Signature Framework Create ONE framework people associate with YOU. Name it. Use it repeatedly. Owned IP = brand moat. your process → name it → repeat → people cite you
40 positioning Origin Story Anchoring Every few weeks re-anchor to your origin story. New followers do not know you. Context makes every other post hit harder. origin story → periodically retell → new audience context
41 positioning Consistent Inconsistency Have a consistent voice but vary content types. Always sound like YOU but surprise with format. Predictable voice unpredictable content. consistent tone → varied format → familiarity + surprise
42 positioning Build In Public Share the messy middle not just polished results. Real-time > highlight reel. real-time update → raw truth → audience invests in journey
43 distribution Golden Hour Posting Each platform has peak engagement windows. Test 3 time slots for 2 weeks. Let data decide YOUR golden hour. platform defaults → test 3 slots → measure → your golden hour
44 distribution Engagement Window Sprint First 60 minutes after posting = critical. Stay online. Reply to EVERY comment within minutes. Algorithm sees early engagement burst → pushes to more feeds. post → 60 min active → reply all → algorithm boost
45 distribution Cross-Pollination Seeding Post on primary platform. Then seed it elsewhere. Screenshot LinkedIn post → Instagram story. Drive traffic both ways. primary post → screenshot/quote → seed on others → cross traffic
46 distribution DM Before Publish Before posting DM 5-10 people who would genuinely care about the topic. Personal touch → guaranteed early engagement. draft ready → 5-10 DMs → genuine interest → early comments
47 distribution Platform Ladder Start content where friction is lowest. Tweet the idea. If traction → LinkedIn post. If that works → Substack deep dive. Let audience validate before you invest more effort. micro test → validate → expand → invest more
48 distribution Batched Creation Separated Publishing Create 5 posts in one creative session. Schedule across the week. Creative energy is bursty. Distribution should be consistent. batch create → queue → drip publish → consistency
49 analytics Post Autopsy Every post that underperforms — WHY? Weak hook? Wrong time? Too long? No CTA? Diagnose ONE cause. Fix it in next post. Never repeat the same death. underperforming post → diagnose → one fix → apply next
50 analytics Winner Pattern Extraction Every month line up your top 5 posts. What do they share? Same hook style? Same pillar? Same length? The pattern IS your playbook. top 5 → common traits → pattern → double down
51 analytics Engagement Decay Tracking Track when engagement peaks and drops off. If comments die after 4 hours your audience is morning-only. Behavior > vanity metrics. time-series data → decay curve → audience behavior insight
52 analytics Framework Effectiveness Score Track which hook/narrative/CTA/tone combos produce highest engagement. After 30 posts you will know YOUR winning formula. hook × narrative × CTA × tone → engagement → ranked combos
53 analytics Audience Growth Forensics When you gain followers trace back — which post brought them? Which posts lose followers? The delta tells you what attracts vs repels. follower spikes → source post → attract patterns → repel patterns
54 analytics Diminishing Returns Detector If same format keeps working but engagement slowly drops — audience fatigue. Track engagement trend PER FORMAT over months. format → monthly engagement trend → plateau signal → rotate
55 creative Constraint Box Set artificial constraints. 'Write this in exactly 5 lines.' '100 words only.' 'No I/me.' Constraints force creativity. Freedom paralyzes. topic → add constraint → forced creativity → surprising output
56 creative Perspective Swap Write your post from someone else's POV. Your manager. Your intern. Your user. Your competitor. Your mother reading your LinkedIn. your topic → someone else's eyes → new angle
57 creative Time Machine Write a letter to past-you or future-you about this topic. Emotional distance creates clarity. topic → past/future self → letter format → emotional depth
58 creative Steal The Structure Find a great post from a DIFFERENT industry. Steal its structure replace with your content. Structure travels content doesn't. other industry post → extract structure → apply your topic
59 creative First Draft Inversion Write the post. Then flip it. If you started with lesson start with story. The inverted version is often better because it surprises even you. first draft → invert structure/tone → compare → pick better
60 creative Shower Thought Capture Best ideas come when you are NOT creating. Gym shower walk chai break. Keep a capture system. Raw thoughts > forced brainstorming. random moment → capture immediately → review weekly → refine into post

FR01 staircase-formatting structure Lines progressively shorter or longer within each block creating a visual staircase effect Line one is the longest in this block.\nThen it gets shorter.\nLike this. All lines are the same length in every block which creates a wall of text that is hard to scan and feels monotonous. P2
FR02 max-three-line-blocks structure No more than 3 consecutive lines without a blank line separating blocks First line of thought.\nSecond line.\nThird line.\n\nNew block starts here. Line one.\nLine two.\nLine three.\nLine four.\nLine five.\nLine six without any break. P1
FR03 no-bold-italic-underline typography Do not use bold italic or underline formatting. Use UPPERCASE sparingly for emphasis on headers only. THE REAL LESSON\n\nHere's what I learned from that experience. **The Real Lesson**\n\n*Here's* what I __learned__ from that experience. P1
FR04 replace-dashes-with-punctuation typography Replace em dashes and en dashes with full stops and commas instead I was tired. Really tired. But I kept going. I was tired — really tired — but I kept going. P1
FR05 bullets-format typography Bullets should use space-hyphen-space format not underscores or other symbols - First point\n - Second point\n - Third point _First point\n_Second point\n• Third point P1
FR06 numbered-lists-format typography Numbered lists should use simple digit-period-space format 1. First item\n2. Second item\n3. Third item 1) First item\n2) Second item\n3) Third item P2
FR07 flows-format typography Sequential flows should use arrow format with spaces A —> B —> C —> D A->B->C->D or A → B → C → D P2
FR08 max-three-emojis emoji Maximum 3 emojis per post placed only at points of maximum emotional tension or buildup Long post with no emojis until... 🔥 the key insight drops. 🚀 Hey everyone! 👋 Today I want to share 💡 my thoughts on 🎯 product management 📊 and why 🔥 it matters 🙏 P1 FR09,max-three-hindi-sentences,language,Maximum 3 Romanised Hindi sentences per post used only at emotional peaks" And that's when it hit me. FR10 fk-grade-7-readability readability Write at FK Grade 7 readability level. Ad copy style with layman language and no jargon. I talked to 50 users. Most said the same thing. FR11 character-limit structure Posts should be 800-1600 ASCII characters in length A well-structured post between 800 and 1600 characters that covers one idea clearly. A 200-character post that says nothing or a 3000-character essay that loses the reader. P1
FR12 footer-line-1-positioning footer After CTA the first footer line should be a positioning statement — who you are and the value you provide PM by day. Builder by night. Writing about what I learn. Follow me for more content! P1
