## How to Select Frameworks for a Post

1. **Start with pillar** — see K6 for which frameworks pair best
2. **Pick content format** — what structure fits the topic?
3. **Pick hook** — what stops the scroll for this topic?
4. **Pick narrative** — what story structure carries the message?
5. **Pick tone** — what voice suits this pillar + topic?
6. **Pick CTA** — what action do you want from the reader?
7. **Pick positioning** — what one-liner fits this post's theme?

**Avoid repetition:** Check last 5 posts' frameworks. Don't reuse the same combo within 7 days.
