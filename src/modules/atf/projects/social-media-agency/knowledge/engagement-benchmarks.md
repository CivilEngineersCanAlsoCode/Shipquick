# LinkedIn Engagement Benchmarks

## Engagement Rate Calculation

```
Engagement Rate = (Reactions + Comments + Reposts) / Impressions × 100
```

Alternative (follower-based):
```
Engagement Rate = (Reactions + Comments + Reposts) / Followers × 100
```

Industry standard uses **impression-based** calculation. Follower-based is useful for comparing across profiles when impression data is unavailable.

## Comment-to-Like Ratio

The comment-to-like ratio is a key quality indicator. Higher ratios signal deeper engagement.

| Ratio | Signal | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| <0.05 (1:20) | Low | Content is "likeable" but not discussion-worthy. Common for motivational quotes, memes |
| 0.05–0.15 (1:20 to 1:7) | Average | Standard professional content. Decent but not driving conversation |
| 0.15–0.30 (1:7 to 1:3) | Strong | Content provokes thought and discussion. Ideal range for thought leadership |
| 0.30–0.50 (1:3 to 1:2) | Excellent | Highly provocative or deeply resonant content. Contrarian takes often land here |
| >0.50 (>1:2) | Exceptional | More comments than half of likes — extremely rare. Usually controversial or asks a direct question |

**Target for Satvik's content**: 0.15–0.30 ratio consistently, with spikes to 0.30+ on contrarian or storytelling posts.

### What Drives High Comment Ratios
- Asking a specific question at the end of the post
- Sharing a contrarian opinion with supporting evidence
- Vulnerability / failure stories that invite "me too" responses
- Framework posts where people share their own versions
- Hot takes on industry trends

## Share Multiplier Effects

Reposts have an outsized impact on reach compared to other engagement types.

| Share Type | Reach Multiplier | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Silent repost | 1.5–2x per share | Minimal — just exposes to sharer's network |
| Repost with commentary | 3–5x per share | Commentary creates a new "post" that distributes independently |
| Share to DM/group | 1.2–1.5x per share | Private but tracked; signals high-value content |
| Share to external | 0.5–1x per share | Lower platform value but can trigger resurgence via inbound traffic |

### Share Velocity Thresholds
- **>5 reposts in first hour**: Almost guarantees Phase 3 distribution
- **>20 reposts in first 24 hours**: Viral trajectory for accounts with <50K followers
- **Repost-to-impression ratio >1.5%**: Top 1% of all LinkedIn content

### Who Shares Matters
- Shares from profiles with >10K followers carry 3–5x the weight
- Shares from profiles in different industries/geographies signal broad relevance
- Shares from LinkedIn Top Voices or verified organizations get algorithmic preference
