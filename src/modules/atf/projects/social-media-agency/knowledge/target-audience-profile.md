# Target Audience Profile

## Satvik's Unique Positioning

**Core narrative**: IIT Delhi engineer → American Express PM → GoGoGo founder (AI-powered) → building in public with deep technical + product + business chops.

### Differentiators
- **IIT Delhi pedigree**: Instant credibility in Indian tech ecosystem; recognized globally by VC and tech communities
- **Amex PM experience**: Enterprise product management at scale — B2B/B2C, compliance-heavy, global teams
- **GoGoGo founder**: Active founder building an AI-first product — not an armchair commentator
- **AI builder**: Hands-on with AI/ML, not just talking about trends — can speak to implementation, architecture, and product decisions
- **Cross-cultural bridge**: Indian education + US corporate + global startup = speaks to multiple audiences authentically

### Positioning Statement
Satvik is a **builder-PM-founder** who combines elite engineering foundations with enterprise product experience and startup founder grit. His content sits at the intersection of **product management, AI/ML, and founder journey** — a unique triangle that very few creators credibly occupy.

## Primary Audience: VCs, Founders with Exits, YCombinator Alumni

### Demographics
- **Location**: SF Bay Area, NYC, Boston, Austin, London, Bangalore
- **Role**: General Partner, Principal, Founder/CEO (post-exit), YC alumni (any batch)
- **Company stage**: Series A–C funds, or post-exit founders advising/investing
- **Age**: 28–50
- **LinkedIn behavior**: Active 3–5x/week, use LinkedIn for deal flow, talent scouting, and thought leadership

### What They Care About
- Market insights that inform investment theses
- Founder stories with real numbers (revenue, churn, CAC, fundraise details)
- AI/ML applications with defensible moats — not "AI wrapper" plays
- Team-building and culture at early-stage companies
- Contrarian takes on hyped markets (what's overvalued, what's undervalued)
- Authentic building-in-public narratives (transparency signals competence to investors)

### Content That Attracts This Segment
| Content Type | Why It Works | Example Hook |
|---|---|---|
| Building in public with metrics | VCs love transparency + data | "Month 6 at GoGoGo: $XX ARR, XX% churn, and the pivot we almost didn't make" |
| AI product architecture decisions | Shows technical depth + product sense | "Why we chose X over Y for our AI pipeline — and the $XX,XXX it saved us" |
| Market analysis with founder lens | Investment-relevant insight | "The AI agent market has a segmentation problem nobody's talking about" |
| Failure/learning stories | Authenticity + pattern matching | "The feature that 50 users requested but killed our retention" |
| Fundraising transparency | Directly relevant to VC workflow | "What I learned from 47 VC meetings in 3 weeks" |

### Posting Strategy
- **Frequency**: 2–3 posts/week targeted at this segment
- **Tone**: Data-driven, specific, no hand-waving. This audience has high BS detectors
- **Format**: Text-heavy posts with specific numbers, occasional carousel for frameworks
- **Engagement**: Comment on VC/founder posts before and after your own posts — this audience is tight-knit and reciprocal
- **Timing**: Tuesday–Thursday, 8:00–9:00 AM PT (matches SF/NYC morning routines)
- **Hashtags**: #startups #venturecapital #ai #founders #buildinpublic
