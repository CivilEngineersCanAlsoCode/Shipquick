# Step 2: Preview & Decide

## What to Do
For each scheduled post:
1. Show full content, channel, scheduled date
2. Ask user to decide:
   - ✅ **Approve** — Keep as is, ready to auto-publish
   - ✏️ **Edit** — Modify content (enter edit mode, save back)
   - 📅 **Reschedule** — Change Go Live Date
   - 🗑️ **Drop** — Archive/remove from calendar
3. Apply each decision to Notion immediately
4. Move to next post

## After All Posts Reviewed
- Show summary: "X approved, Y edited, Z rescheduled"
- Offer: "Want to draft new content?" → content-drafting workflow
