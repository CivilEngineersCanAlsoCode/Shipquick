# LinkedIn Post Template — Format Reference

This document defines the exact structural template for LinkedIn posts formatted by the F-ContentFormatting workflow. Every formatted post MUST follow this structure. Deviations are formatting violations.

---

## Template Anatomy

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  HOOK (Line 1)                              │
│  < 210 chars, above "see more" fold         │
│  Style: curiosity / data / controversial    │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                             │
│  BODY                                       │
│  3-line blocks, staircase pattern           │
│  UPPERCASE headers (2-3 max)                │
│  Emojis at tension points (0-3 max)         │
│  Hindi at emotional peaks (0-3 max)         │
│                                             │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  KEY TAKEAWAY                               │
│  1-2 lines, the bold insight                │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  CTA                                        │
│  Question or engagement prompt              │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  POSITIONING LINE                           │
│  Who you are + value you provide            │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  FOLLOW LINE                                │
│  Invitation to follow                       │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                             │
│  HASHTAGS (3-6, generic, at very end)       │
│                                             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## Section-by-Section Rules

### 1. Hook (First Line)

The hook is the MOST important line. It appears before the "see more" fold on LinkedIn (~210 characters). If the hook fails, nobody reads the rest.

**Constraints:**
- Maximum 210 characters (to stay above the "see more" threshold)
- Must be 1 line (2 lines absolute max)
- Standalone sentence, not a lead-in to the next paragraph

**Hook Styles:**
| Style | Pattern | Example |
|-------|---------|---------|
| Curiosity | Open a loop the reader must close | "I deleted my best-performing feature. Here's why." |
| Data | Lead with a specific number or stat | "3 features. 6 months. Zero users." |
| Controversial | Challenge a common belief | "Most founders fail at content. Not because they can't write." |
| Pattern Interrupt | Say something unexpected | "I stopped hiring senior engineers." |
| Direct Address | Speak to the reader | "You're probably building the wrong thing right now." |

### 2. Body (3-Line Blocks, Staircase Pattern)

The body is structured as a series of short blocks, each separated by a blank line. The visual effect is a "staircase" — the reader's eye flows naturally downward.

**Constraints:**
- Maximum 3 consecutive lines per block (HARD rule, FR02)
- Each block is one distinct thought
- Blank line between every block
- Lines within a block can vary in length (progressively shorter or longer = staircase)
- No wall-of-text dumps

**UPPERCASE Headers:**
- Used to mark section transitions within the body
- Maximum 2-3 per post
- Full caps, standalone line: `HERE'S WHAT CHANGED`
- Never for emphasis within sentences: "The MOST important thing" is WRONG

**Formatting within body:**
- Bullets: ` - ` (space-hyphen-space)
- Flow arrows: `A —> B —> C` (em-dash + greater-than)
- No bold, italic, underline, or rich Unicode characters
- All dashes replaced with proper punctuation (periods, commas, colons, parentheses)

### 3. Key Takeaway

The distilled insight from the post. This is the "so what?" moment.

**Constraints:**
- 1-2 lines maximum
- Clear, memorable phrasing
- Should work as a standalone insight even without the rest of the post
- Often preceded by an UPPERCASE header like `THE REAL LESSON` or `HERE'S THE THING`

### 4. CTA (Call to Action)

A question or engagement prompt that invites the reader to respond.

**Constraints:**
- 1-2 lines
- Must be a genuine question or prompt (not a demand)
- Should relate to the post topic
- Examples: "What's one feature you built that nobody used?", "Drop your biggest lesson in the comments."

### 5. Positioning Line

One sentence establishing who you are and the value you provide.

**Constraints:**
- Exactly 1 sentence
- Answers: "Who is this person and why should I follow them?"
- Consistent across posts (builds brand recognition)
- Confident but not salesy

### 6. Follow Line

A direct invitation to follow for more content.

**Constraints:**
- Exactly 1 sentence
- Specific about what the reader gets by following
- Action-oriented

### 7. Hashtags

Generic, topic-relevant hashtags placed at the very end.

**Constraints:**
- 3-6 hashtags (no fewer, no more)
- After the follow line, separated by one blank line
- `#CamelCase` format
- Mix broad + niche
- Never embedded in body text

## Character Budget

| Section | Approximate Characters |
|---------|----------------------|
| Hook | 50-210 |
| Body (all blocks) | 500-1100 |
| Key Takeaway | 50-150 |
| CTA | 30-100 |
| Positioning + Follow | 60-150 |
| Hashtags | 30-80 |
| Blank lines / whitespace | ~50-80 |
| **Total** | **800-1600** |

```
Your best feature idea is hiding in your users' complaints.
Not in your roadmap.

What's one feature you built that nobody used?

I build AI products solo and share what actually works.
Follow for no-fluff lessons from the trenches. 🚀

#ProductManagement #SoloFounder #BuildInPublic #Startup #AI
```

## Anti-Patterns (What NOT to Do)

```
BAD: Wall of text with no breaks
───────────────────────────────────
I shipped 3 features last year. All three flopped. Not a single user
asked for them. I built what I thought was cool, not what users needed.
This was a massive lesson in product management. I learned that you
should always talk to users before building. The data was clear. Nobody
wanted what I was making. So I changed my approach completely.

BAD: Emoji overload
───────────────────────────────────
🚀 Hey everyone! 👋 Today I want to share 💡 my thoughts on
🎯 product management 📊 and why 🔥 we should all 💪 build better!

BAD: Bold/italic/underline (LinkedIn won't render it)
───────────────────────────────────
**Here's What I Learned**
*Simple* sentence about the __lesson__.

BAD: Wrong bullet/arrow formats
───────────────────────────────────
• First point
* Second point
→ Third point
Idea -> Prototype -> Ship

BAD: Hashtags in body text
───────────────────────────────────
I learned about #ProductManagement the hard way.
My #startup journey taught me that #BuildInPublic is key.
```
