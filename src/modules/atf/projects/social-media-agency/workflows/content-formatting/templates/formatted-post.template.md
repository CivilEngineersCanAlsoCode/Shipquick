# Formatted LinkedIn Post Template

Use this structure when formatting a drafted post for LinkedIn. Every section is intentional — do not skip or reorder.

---

## Template Structure

```
{HOOK_LINE}

{BODY_BLOCK_1}
(max 3 lines)

{BODY_BLOCK_2}
(max 3 lines)

{BODY_BLOCK_3}
(max 3 lines, optional Hindi sentence here if emotional peak)

{BODY_BLOCK_4}
(max 3 lines, continue as needed)

{OPTIONAL_SECTION_HEADER_UPPERCASE}

{BODY_BLOCK_5}
(max 3 lines)

{CTA_POSITIONING}
{CTA_FOLLOW_PROMPT}

{HASHTAGS}
```

---

## Section Guide

### Hook Line
- First line the reader sees. Must grab attention instantly.
- Options: UPPERCASE statement, emoji-led opener, provocative question, bold claim
- Keep it to 1 line (2 max)
- Example: `Most founders fail at content. Not because they can't write.`

### Body Blocks
- Each block: 1-3 lines of text
- Separated by blank lines (staircase layout)
- Use ` - ` for bullets within blocks
- Use `A —> B —> C` for process flows
- No dashes (replace with periods, commas, colons)
- Simple words, short sentences (FK Grade 7)
- Max 3 emojis across ALL blocks (at tension points only)
- Max 3 Hindi sentences across ALL blocks (at emotional peaks only)

### UPPERCASE Headers (Optional)
- Use for section breaks within the post
- Maximum 2-3 per post
- Example: `HERE'S THE THING`
- Do NOT use UPPERCASE for emphasis within sentences

### CTA (Call to Action)
- Two parts, both required:
  1. **Positioning:** One sentence about who you are / what you do
  2. **Follow prompt:** Tell the reader to follow for more
- Example:
  ```
  I build AI products as a solo founder and share what I learn along the way.
  Follow for daily lessons from the trenches.
  ```

### Hashtags
- 3-6 hashtags, separated by spaces
- Placed after the CTA, separated by one blank line
- Relevant to post topic and LinkedIn audience
- Example: `#AI #SoloFounder #ContentStrategy #LinkedIn #BuildInPublic`

---

## Character Count Reference
- Minimum: 800 ASCII characters
- Maximum: 1600 ASCII characters
- Count includes ALL text (hook, body, CTA, hashtags, blank lines)
- If under 800: expand a key point or add an example
- If over 1600: tighten sentences, remove filler, trim redundant blocks

```
Because they don't know WHO they're writing for.

I spent 6 months posting on LinkedIn.
Zero traction.
Zero DMs.

Then I did one thing differently.

I stopped writing for "everyone."
I picked ONE person.
My ideal customer.

Every post became a conversation with that person.

HERE'S WHAT CHANGED

 - Engagement went up 3x
 - DMs started flowing in
 - Clients found ME instead of the other way around

Yeh sach mein game changer tha. 🔥

The formula is simple:
Pick one person —> Write for them —> Repeat

Stop broadcasting. Start connecting.

I build AI products solo and share what actually works.
Follow for no-fluff lessons from the trenches. 🚀

#SoloFounder #LinkedIn #ContentStrategy #BuildInPublic #AI
```
