# Content Ideation

**Goal:** Generate a batch of content ideas aligned with the user's brand, audience, and strategic goals.

**System:** This is a BMAD workflow — interactive, human-in-the-loop. The AI collaborates with the user, NOT automates away from them.

---

## Steps

1. **Gather Context** — Check Notion Garden of Ideas, recent posts, current themes
2. **Brainstorm** — Generate 5-10 ideas with user input, mix AI suggestions with user's raw thoughts
3. **Filter & Prioritize** — User picks winners, rates relevance, assigns channels
4. **Save to Notion** — Push approved ideas to Garden of Ideas or Content Calendar

## Inputs
- Notion Garden of Ideas database (`3f41482c-cd34-826e-8de1-01bb53f385df`)
- Notion Content Calendar (`cc01482c-cd34-83ac-81a8-013e4d767924`)
- User's current mood, trending topics, business priorities

## Outputs
- 3-5 approved content ideas saved to Notion
- Each idea tagged with channel, format, and priority
