# Step 2: Brainstorm

## What to Do
1. Based on context from Step 1, generate 5-10 content ideas
2. Mix formats: threads, short posts, long-form, video ideas, polls
3. Mix channels: LinkedIn, Telegram, YouTube
4. Present ideas with: Title, Hook (first line), Channel, Format
5. Ask user to react: 👍 keep, 👎 drop, ✏️ modify

## Brainstorm Framework
- **Personal stories** — Career lessons, failures, wins
- **Industry insights** — Product management, startups, tech
- **Hot takes** — Contrarian views, unpopular opinions  
- **How-to** — Tactical advice, frameworks, templates
- **Behind-the-scenes** — Day in life, building GoGoGo, Amex work (non-confidential)

## Tone Guidelines
- Casual + witty (default)
- English only
- Authentic > polished

## Output
- User-approved list of 3-5 ideas with channel assignments
