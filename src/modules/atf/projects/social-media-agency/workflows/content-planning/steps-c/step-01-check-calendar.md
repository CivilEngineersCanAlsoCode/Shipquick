# Step 1: Check Calendar

## What to Do
1. Ask user: "Planning for this week or next week? Or whole month?"
2. Query Notion Content Calendar for the chosen period
3. Show current state:
   - Filled slots (date + title + channel)
   - Empty days (gaps)
   - Ideas in pipeline (status: Idea/Drafting)
4. Show ideas from Garden of Ideas that could fill gaps

## Output
- Calendar overview with gaps highlighted
- Available ideas to slot in
