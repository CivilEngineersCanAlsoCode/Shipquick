# Content Planning

**Goal:** Plan a week or month of content by mapping ideas to dates and channels.

**System:** BMAD workflow — interactive. User decides the strategy, AI helps structure it.

---

## Steps

1. **Check Calendar** — See what's already scheduled, find gaps
2. **Map Content** — Assign ideas from Garden/Calendar to specific dates
3. **Balance** — Ensure mix of channels, formats, topics
4. **Confirm & Save** — Lock in the plan to Notion

## Posting Frequency
- LinkedIn: 1/day
- Telegram: 1/day
- YouTube: 1/week

## Inputs
- Notion Content Calendar (existing schedule)
- Notion Garden of Ideas (available ideas)
- User's priorities for the period

## Outputs
- Complete content calendar for chosen period
- Each slot: Title, Channel, Date, Status
