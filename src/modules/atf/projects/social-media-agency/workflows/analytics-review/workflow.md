# Analytics Review

**Goal:** Review how published content performed and adjust strategy based on data.

**System:** BMAD workflow — interactive. AI presents data, user draws conclusions, together they adjust.

---

## Steps

1. **Fetch Metrics** — Pull analytics from Notion KPIs database
2. **Analyze** — Highlight top performers, underperformers, trends
3. **Discuss** — User reflects on what worked and why
4. **Adjust Strategy** — Update approach for next cycle
