# Step 2: Discuss & Adjust

## What to Do
1. Ask user: "What do you think worked? Anything surprise you?"
2. AI shares observations:
   - Which topics got most engagement
   - Which channels performed best
   - Posting time patterns
   - Content format trends
3. Together decide adjustments:
   - More of what works, less of what doesn't
   - New topics to explore
   - Channel focus shifts
4. Capture insights for next content-planning cycle

## Output
- Key insights documented
- Strategy adjustments noted
- Suggest: "Want to plan next week's content based on these insights?" → content-planning workflow
