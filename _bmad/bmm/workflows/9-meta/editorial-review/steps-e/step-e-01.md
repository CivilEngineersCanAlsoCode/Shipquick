# Step E1: Initial Copy-Edit

## STEP GOAL:

Identify and communicate clarity and prose issues in the target document.

## MANDATORY SEQUENCE:

1. **Draft Selection**: Ask which document or section needs review.
2. **Prose Check**: Identify passive voice, jargon, and bloated sentences.
3. **Suggestions**: Present a list of proposed edits.

## MENU OPTIONS:

[C] Apply Edits — Update the document
[B] Back — Cancel review
