# Workflow: Adversarial Review

## OBJECTIVE

Cynically challenge content to surface critical flaws and improvement areas.

## PHASES

1. **Critical Audit**: Find 3-10 specific problems in the provided content.
2. **Severity Assessment**: Categorize findings by impact (High/Medium/Low).
3. **Resolution Planning**: Propose fixes for identified issues.
