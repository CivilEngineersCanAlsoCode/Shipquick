# Step E1: Critical Challenge

## STEP GOAL:

Systematically search for weaknesses, contradictions, and omissions.

## MANDATORY SEQUENCE:

1. **Target Identification**: Specify the component or document to audit.
2. **Adversarial Analysis**: Generate 5 critical questions or problems.
3. **Evidence Collection**: Link findings to specific lines or requirements.

## MENU OPTIONS:

[C] Generate Report — Finalize findings
[B] Back — Cancel audit
