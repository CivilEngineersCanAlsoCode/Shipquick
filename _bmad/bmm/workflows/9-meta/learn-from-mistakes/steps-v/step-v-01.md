# Step V1: Knowledge Verification

## STEP GOAL:

Ensure that mistakes are correctly documented and prioritized.

## MANDATORY SEQUENCE:

1. **Duplicate Check**: Verify the learning hasn't already been documented.
2. **Actionability Check**: Confirm the "Prevention Action" is specific and enforceable.
3. **Persistence Check**: Confirm both `Gemini.md` and `global-learnings.md` are updated.

## MENU OPTIONS:

[C] Finish — Exit learning mode
[R] Revise — Refine documented learning
