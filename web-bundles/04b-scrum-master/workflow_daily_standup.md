# Daily Standup Workflow

## Purpose

A focused 15-minute daily synchronization to inspect progress toward the Iteration Goal and adapt the plan for the next 24 hours.

## Pre-Conditions

- Team has an active Iteration with committed stories.
- A visible board (physical or digital) showing work items.

## Workflow Steps

### Step 1: Opening (1 min)

- SM starts on time, no waiting for latecomers.
- Quick reminder: "We're here to sync, not report status."

### Step 2: Walk the Board (10 min)

- **Right to Left**: Start from the rightmost column (closest to Done).
- For each item in progress, the owner answers:
  1. "What did I complete since yesterday?"
  2. "What will I work on today?"
  3. "Is anything blocking me?"
- **Focus on flow**: If an item hasn't moved, ask why.
- **WIP Limits**: If WIP is exceeded, discuss which item to pause.

### Step 3: Blockers & Parking Lot (3 min)

- SM captures any blockers on the impediment backlog.
- Topics requiring deeper discussion go to the **Parking Lot** (after standup).
- SM assigns owners to parking lot items.

### Step 4: Close (1 min)

- SM confirms: "Any last blockers? No? Great, let's go build."
- Immediately follow up on parking lot items with relevant people.

## Anti-Patterns to Watch For

- **Round-Robin Reporting**: People report to the SM instead of each other → Walk the board instead.
- **Problem-Solving in Standup**: Deep technical discussions → Park them.
- **Running Over 15 Minutes**: Strict timeboxing → SM cuts off politely.
- **Skipping When SM is Absent**: Team should run standup without the SM.

## Output

- Updated impediment backlog.
- Parking lot items with owners.
- Team awareness of daily priorities.
