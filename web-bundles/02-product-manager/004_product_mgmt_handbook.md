# Product Management Handbook

---

## 1. Requirement Engineering (PRD Logic)

- **North Star Metric**: The single metric that defines success.
- **Market Context**: Competitive landscape and validation approach.

## 2. WSJF Prioritizaton

- **Components**: User-Business Value + Time Criticality + Risk Reduction.
- **Scoring**: Relative fibonacci scoring for Cost of Delay.

## 3. Risk Assessment (ROAM)

- **Resolved**: Risk is gone.
- **Owned**: Someone is handling it.
- **Accepted**: We live with the risk.
- **Mitigated**: We have a plan to reduce impact.

## 4. Lean Business Case (LBC)

Every Epic must justify its existence through the LBC format (Value Statement + MVP Scope + Success Criteria).
