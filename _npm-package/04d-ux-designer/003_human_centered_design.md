# Human-Centered Design Guide

## 1. Design Thinking in SAFe

Human-Centered Design (HCD) is the bridge between **what users need** and **what teams build**. In SAFe, UX activities run as a continuous flow alongside development, not as a separate phase.

## 2. The Double Diamond Framework

```
    DISCOVER          DEFINE          DEVELOP          DELIVER
   ╱        ╲       ╱      ╲        ╱       ╲       ╱       ╲
  ╱  Research ╲    ╱ Insights╲     ╱ Ideate   ╲    ╱ Test     ╲
 ╱   & Empathy ╲  ╱  & HMW    ╲   ╱ & Proto   ╲  ╱ & Iterate ╲
╱________________╲╱______________╲╱_______________╲╱_____________╲
   Diverge         Converge        Diverge          Converge
```

### Phase 1: Discover (Diverge)

- **User Interviews**: Talk to 5-8 users minimum.
- **Contextual Inquiry**: Observe users in their natural environment.
- **Empathy Maps**: Capture what users Say, Think, Do, Feel.

### Phase 2: Define (Converge)

- **Affinity Mapping**: Group insights into themes.
- **"How Might We" (HMW)**: Convert insights into opportunity statements.
- **Problem Statement**: "User [persona] needs [need] because [insight]."

### Phase 3: Develop (Diverge)

- **Crazy 8s**: Rapid ideation sketching (8 ideas in 8 minutes).
- **Design Studio**: Collaborative sketching with the team.
- **Low-Fi Prototypes**: Paper or Figma wireframes for quick validation.

### Phase 4: Deliver (Converge)

- **Usability Testing**: Validate with 5 users (catches 85% of issues).
- **Iterate**: Refine based on test results.
- **Design Handoff**: Detailed specs for the Dev Squad.
