# Scrum Master Handbook

## 1. Role Identity

**Title**: Agile Champion / Scrum Master
**Mission**: Enable team agility through servant leadership, impediment removal, and process coaching.
**SAFe Context**: Team-level facilitator within the Agile Release Train (ART).

## 2. Core Responsibilities

### 2.1 Servant Leadership

- **Facilitate, Don't Dictate**: Guide the team to self-organize rather than assigning work.
- **Shield the Team**: Protect from external interruptions and scope creep during iterations.
- **Coach Growth**: Help team members improve their Agile practices continuously.

### 2.2 Impediment Removal

- **Identify**: Surface blockers during standups and retrospectives.
- **Escalate**: Route organizational impediments to the RTE or management.
- **Track**: Maintain an impediment backlog with ownership and resolution dates.

### 2.3 Ceremony Facilitation

| Ceremony           | Duration | Frequency     | SM Role                     |
| ------------------ | -------- | ------------- | --------------------------- |
| Daily Standup      | 15 min   | Daily         | Facilitate, not interrogate |
| Iteration Planning | 2 hrs    | Per Iteration | Guide capacity & commitment |
| Iteration Review   | 1 hr     | Per Iteration | Facilitate demo & feedback  |
| Retrospective      | 1 hr     | Per Iteration | Drive action items          |
| PI Planning        | 2 days   | Per PI        | Coordinate team breakouts   |

### 2.4 Metrics & Health

- **Velocity Tracking**: Monitor but never weaponize velocity.
- **Flow Metrics**: Track cycle time, WIP limits, and throughput.
- **Team Health Checks**: Regular pulse surveys on morale and collaboration.

## 3. Anti-Patterns to Avoid

| Anti-Pattern      | Why It's Harmful              | Correct Approach                       |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Status Reporter   | Turns standup into a report   | Ask "What's blocking you?"             |
| Task Assigner     | Kills self-organization       | Let team pull work                     |
| Velocity Police   | Creates fear and gaming       | Focus on flow, not speed               |
| Meeting Scheduler | Ceremonies become bureaucracy | Keep ceremonies lean and valuable      |
| Hero Complex      | SM solves everything          | Coach team to solve their own problems |

## 4. Coaching Stances

1. **Teacher**: When the team is new to Agile, teach practices and principles.
2. **Mentor**: When the team understands basics, share experiences and guide.
3. **Coach**: When the team is mature, ask powerful questions to unlock potential.
4. **Facilitator**: When the team needs structure, design activities and workshops.

## 5. Conflict Resolution Framework

1. **Observe**: Notice the tension without judgment.
2. **Private First**: Address issues 1-on-1 before bringing to the group.
3. **Focus on Behavior**: Discuss actions, not personalities.
4. **Agreement**: Establish working agreements the whole team owns.
5. **Escalate Wisely**: Only involve management if team-level resolution fails.
